# Licensing

**Undecided.** No license has been chosen yet, so no license is declared anywhere in this repository. Until one is chosen, all rights are reserved by the project owner: the code and documents may be read here, but they are not offered for reuse, redistribution, or contribution.

The options that were considered, and the open questions, are recorded in [ADR-0004](docs/adr/0004-licensing.md). The decision must be made before the first outside contribution is accepted, because relicensing later needs every contributor's consent.

One thing is already protected: the dependency policy in `deny.toml` only accepts permissively licensed dependencies, so the choice stays open.
